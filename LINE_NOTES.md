# Line notes -- what the field does, and what was cut

Measured, not guessed: 25 distinct judged entries read at 17:30Z on 25 November from the bounty's
own public submissions list (`/api/competitions/<slug>/submissions`, artifact.txt of every entry
with `status: finished`; 158 of the newest 400 were finished, first 25 texts read, 25 distinct --
the field does not copy itself yet).

## What the field saturates

- **The defect-report register.** "Unit may …", "side effects include …", "degraded operation …":
  at least 11 of the 25 open on a device-defect clause. It is the default move, so a line in it has
  to beat eleven siblings on wording alone.
- **Saying everything is fine while visibly failing.** "I'm fine" while on fire (twice), "totally
  fine right up until catastrophic failure", "good, you?" under all conditions.
- **Composition.** "70 % water, 100 % opinions" (twice), "contains an opinion formed in under two
  seconds".
- **Two-hour research, one-second commitment.** Toaster reviews against a 30-year mortgage.
- **Rehearsed confrontation then "no worries at all"**, and **cleaning the house instead of the
  one task**.

## What none of them used

The **printed hardware label**, quoted whole: the line on a boogie board that says it is not a
flotation device. The judge's criterion 4 rewards comedy that comes from the form it imitates, and
this is a form the field left alone.

## Candidates, with the reason each was cut

1. `WARNING: Do not use the human as a flotation device. It will hold you up, then bring it up at your wedding.` -- **kept** (107 chars).
2. `DANGER: LIGHT FUSE AND GET AWAY. The human will light it and lean in for a better photo.` -- cut: the label is addressed to a fireworks user, so criterion 1 (a label for humans) is arguable, and arguable criterion 1 loses.
3. `SURGEON GENERAL'S WARNING: Leaving the group chat greatly reduces the risk of a 60-message argument about where to eat.` -- cut: strong form, but the turn is a mild observation; the kept line's turn is a second idea in the last three words.
4. `WARNING: Hot surface. The human will test it with the back of one finger and report the result as a surprise.` -- cut: true and clean, but the form is thin and the punch is a description rather than a turn.
5. `CAUTION: Never stand on the top step. The human will stand on the top step, call it a shortcut, and film it.` -- cut: three beats where the line has room for one; the third beat is filler under criterion 5.
6. `WARNING: Choking hazard. Small parts. The human will swallow the headline whole and repeat it at dinner as research.` -- cut: two ideas (choking, then dinner-table expertise), and "as research" is an explainer for the joke.
7. `WARNING: Do not remove this tag under penalty of law. The human has kept the tag in a drawer for nine years.` -- cut: recognition without a turn.

## Why the kept line should win a pair

- Criterion 1: it opens on `WARNING:` and its subject is a human being, in the words.
- Criterion 2: the turn is the last three words, `at your wedding`; nothing before it explains itself.
- Criterion 3: it is recognition -- the person who helps you once and never lets it go -- not
  absurdity.
- Criterion 4: `not a flotation device` is furniture printed on real packaging, so the rhythm is
  borrowed rather than invented.
- Criterion 5: 107 of 160 characters, one idea, no second gag, no address to the judge.
